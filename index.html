Pretty is easy.

I'm Ruhan, a Shopify and WordPress developer from Sylhet, Bangladesh. I started freelancing in 2024 and have been building websites and online stores for clients around the world ever since.

From 2024 to 2025 I was a Freelance Developer, working on projects for clients in the US, UK and Europe. It taught me what shipping at scale really takes: process, reviews and accountability.

In 2025 I founded Ruhan Studio, a founder-led Shopify & WordPress studio. As Founder & CTO I scope the work, write the code and stay after launch. Fast pages, clean structure, honest advice and clear pricing are the whole point.

Away from client work I run Ruhan Academy, a computer training institute in Sylhet, and you'll find me at WordCamps and local web, design and AI meetups whenever I can make it.

——   06   -   FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What clients usually ask before we start working together.

What does Razibur Rahman do?

+

I'm a Shopify and WordPress developer and the Founder & CTO of WebVines. I build custom Shopify stores and WordPress / WooCommerce websites, migrate sites between platforms, speed up slow sites, and add SEO, accessibility and AI features. I've been building for the web since 2017.

Can you migrate my store or website without losing SEO?

+

Yes, I've moved stores and sites from custom platforms, Wix and older themes to Shopify and WordPress. Every migration includes a URL map with 301 redirects, preserved page titles and meta, and checks in Google Search Console after launch, so rankings carry over.

Do you work with clients in the US, UK and Europe?

+

Yes. Most of my clients are in the US and UK. I work from Sylhet, Bangladesh (GMT+6), share progress in writing every working day and schedule calls in your time zone.

Can you make my Shopify or WordPress site faster?

+

Yes. I start with a speed audit, then fix what actually slows the site: heavy themes and page builders, unused apps and plugins, unoptimised images and render-blocking scripts. The goal is passing Core Web Vitals on mobile and desktops.

Do you build AI features and automation?

+

Yes. I add AI chat assistants, AI-assisted product and content workflows, and automations that connect your store or website to the tools you already use, so routine work happens without manual steps.

How long does a project take and how much does it cost?

+

It depends on scope. After a short call you get a written proposal with a fixed price, a timeline and exactly what's included, before any work starts.

How do I start a project?

+

Email razibur@webvines.com or book a free 30-minute call. Tell me what you sell, what isn't working and what you'd like the site to do, and I'll reply within one business day.
